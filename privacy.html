Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

At SS GAMES ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our website visitors and players. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we may collect, how that information is used, and your rights regarding your data when using our gaming portal.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

SS GAMES is designed as an open-access web gaming portal. We do not require you to create an account, register your personal phone number, or provide credit card information to play our core browser games.

3. Local Device Storage (Browser Storage)

Our games may use standard browser technologies such as localStorage or sessionStorage solely to store your local high scores, game progression, or preferred sound settings on your device. This data remains on your browser and is never transmitted or sold to any external servers.

4. Advertising and Future Monetization

As SS GAMES continues to scale, we may integrate reputable third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdSense) to keep our games free for players globally. These advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar tracking technologies to serve non-personalized or personalized advertisements compliant with standard data protection regulations. When active, these networks adhere to their own distinct privacy guidelines.

5. Third-Party Links & External Content

Our platform may feature links to external websites, developer portals, or partner tools. SS GAMES does not control and is not liable for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms.

6. Children's Privacy

SS GAMES does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take necessary steps to remove it.

7. Contact and Inquiries
